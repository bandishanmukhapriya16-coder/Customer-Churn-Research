3. INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------
Bootstrap intervals estimate uncertainty in the observed test-set metrics.
McNemar's test compares paired classification errors between two models.
Bonferroni-adjusted p-values account for the multiple pairwise comparisons.
A p-value below 0.05 after correction indicates evidence of different error patterns under this test.
Statistical significance does not necessarily mean the performance difference is practically important.
These findings are conditional on this particular train-test split and should be validated on independent data.
